This is something I should have probably done a while back as a Canadian software developer, and now I've finally done it.

You can now browse my website at [https://thaniri.ca](https://thaniri.ca) instead of the old `.com` domain. I'm also going through moving my email domain, but that will take longer to shake out as there are lots of accounts associated with my old email.

The motivation behind this was to move my digital footprint away from the United States. We know about things like NSA spying, the Patriot Act, and rampant surveillance capitalism going on in the United States and I'm electing to stop feeding so many dollars into that system. Digital privacy matters, and we shouldn't be paying to be turned into products to be farmed by tech companies.

As of today, am I a pure FOSS and privacy zealot that is unable to compromise? No. This website is still hosted by AWS (albeit in Canada). I know that the CLOUD Act means the United States government can compel an American tech company to hand over private data that is stored outside of the US, but an S3 hosted static website is still remarkably cheap and easy to run. It shows that I still know my way around building things in AWS which is still a sought-after skill even in the age LLMs.

I have other, more critical, parts of my digital footprint which indeed are quite important to make sure they're in well protected jurisdictions in the world, but this blog is not that important. So this website will continue to be hosted in AWS for now, as it serves its purpose well enough.
