I am a software professional. I design, implement, deploy, and maintain software systems.

This blog is my outlet to think and build in public. As LLMs disrupt knowledge work, I believe that clarity, judgement, and depth of thought are going to be the differentiating skills for knowledge workers, and writing is one of the best ways to practice them. As such, I can honestly say that none of the posts on this blog are LLM written or will ever be LLM written. That would defeat my purpose for making content entirely.

This website is ad-free and cookie free, and I would like to keep it that way. My content will always be free to distribute and never be behind a paywall.
